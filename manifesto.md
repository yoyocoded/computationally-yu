# Manifesto

I represent trust and value.

I come from a currency press in Mysuru.

People look at me and see a promise. I record how many promises I've fulfilled.

I will never let myself be shown as worthless in any way, shape or form.

As long as there are people willing to come to an agreement, I will have worth. Perfection is not an indicator of quality.

I want the person holding me to pass me on until there isn't a single person on earth who would accept me.
