# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1 · 20, a 20-rupee note

20 is a 20-rupee note that has changed countless hands. It's set in the future, when the shift from paper money to electronic systems is more prominent and people find less and less reasons to use a 20 note. Where does all the paper money end up going when we shift? When does it lose value? What is the value of money? As 20 meets less and less people, it folds, crumples, tears, comes undone. Much like :) the economy :)

## 2 · Masami, a cell tower

Masami is a cell tower set in the present, in a relatively remote part of Bangalore, one whose main attractions are the farmlands and an International School. It is the only one in its 1.5 km radius. It stands alone, but it has seen millions of stories, messages, people, entire lives. It has never seen the next cell tower. Nor will it ever. Its only job is to relay information quietly. What is it like being so connected but so isolated? The more signals improve, the further apart cell towers will become. Does this mean something? :0

## 3 · Sirohi, a high-ranking soldier's sword

Sirohi, a high-ranking soldier's sword from the past. The hilt was polished brass and adorned with silver inlay. The high-carbon steel blade drew blood from the men it encountered just before it drew out their very souls. At night, it would lie dormant in its scabbard, on a platform right next to its owner. He cared for Sirohi like it was a piece of fine jewellery, but when war arose once more, the polished brass would bear stains of dark, rich blood. We ascribe glory to violence. We decorate harbingers of destruction. Why do we dress up when we kill? Is it to make the violence less repugnant? So we feel less guilty? Does it comfort us?
